# Health facilities, Kisarawe district

`kisarawe.csv` is filtered from "Health Facilities in Tanzania" (Tanzania Health Facility Registry
snapshot, June 2016) published on openAFRICA under Creative Commons Attribution:
https://open.africa/dataset/health-facilities-in-tanzania

Columns: facility_id, name, common_name, region, district, council, ward, village, level,
operating_status, ownership, lat, lon, source_updated. Imported as the `health` domain baseline
(status `open`, channel `import`, low weight) by `reach.ingest.health`.

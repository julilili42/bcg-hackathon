# Simulated WaterLine news call

This recording simulates an outbound notification. The application still responds to an
incoming call or web conversation; it does not initiate calls. Label the presentation
“Simulated proactive notification · fictional demo data”. No application changes are required.

The fictional community notice was published on October 17, 2026. It announces an extra
collection session on the morning of October 18 at the Alero school borehole. It is unverified
and does not certify water quality or change the water point's reported condition.

1. Start a call or the web voice demo and record the opening greeting.
2. Ask: **“What is the new community announcement about the extra collection session at the Alero school borehole?”**
3. Cut your question and the waiting time. Keep the greeting followed by the agent's announcement.
   Expect a brief update about tomorrow morning's collection session, qualified as a community notice.
4. Keep your follow-up audible: **“For the extra collection session at the Alero school borehole, where should I go and what should I bring?”**
   Expect the school gate, help from the caretaker, and your own clean water container.

Use the full water-point name in the follow-up because the application does not retain
conversation context. Replies are generated, so wording may vary. The notice has a fixed
date: on a later recording day, the agent should describe its date accurately rather than
keep calling it “tomorrow”.

The source data is in `demo_data/alero_news_bulletin.json`, stored in `water_knowledge`
under `demo-outreach-alero-collection-notice-v1`. Existing statuses and report history are preserved.
